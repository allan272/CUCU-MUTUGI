8.2  Key Design Principles
Mobile-first — over 80% of Kenyan farmers will visit on a smartphone
Large, clear call-to-action buttons (Order Chicks, WhatsApp Us, Get Support)
High-quality photos of real chickens, real farms, real Kenyan farmers
Green and gold palette signals agriculture, trust, and prosperity
Swahili language option — consider adding a KE language toggle
Fast loading — optimize all images; farmers may have slower connections
Every page should have a WhatsApp floating button
